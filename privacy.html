Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Core Commitment to Privacy

PixelCraft ("we", "our", or "the platform") is engineered with a strict client-first privacy architecture. Most of our tools process digital assets—including photos, cropped images, color data, text transformations, and QR codes—completely inside your local browser memory using modern web standards.

2. Information We Collect

For guest users, we do not require any personal identification, registration, or contact information. When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, password hash (salted using bcrypt), and optional creative usage preferences.

3. Document & File Handling

Files processed through client-side tools are not stored on our servers. Files uploaded for explicit cloud projects (such as saved designs and invoice drafts) are isolated using strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies and accessible only to the authenticated creator.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your creations. You can export or permanently delete your account and saved projects at any time through Account Settings.
