Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, privacy, and security.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time with one click from your user settings. When you cancel, you will retain all Pro or Business capabilities until the end of your prepaid billing period. No lock-in, no hidden cancellation fees.
What happens to my saved projects if my subscription expires?
Your project data is never deleted! If your subscription ends, your account moves gracefully to the Free tier. All existing projects remain securely intact in your workspace, and you can view, export, or edit them at any time.
Are my images and PDF documents uploaded to external servers?
No. PixelCraft is engineered with strict client-side zero-knowledge architecture. Image compression, PDF merging, QR creation, and canvas rendering execute entirely in your local browser memory using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Your files never touch our servers.
What payment methods are supported?
We support all instant payment rails: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), major Credit and Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), and Netbanking with instantaneous automated license activation and GST-compliant tax invoices.
How does the 20% Annual Discount work?
When you switch to the Yearly billing cycle above, Pro pricing drops from ₹149/mo to ₹119/mo (billed annually at ₹1,428), and Business pricing drops from ₹399/mo to ₹319/mo (billed annually at ₹3,828) — giving you 2 full months completely free.
