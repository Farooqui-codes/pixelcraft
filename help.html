Help Centre & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using PixelCraft's creative tools, project workspaces, and billing.

Are my uploaded files and images stored on your servers?

No! By default, PixelCraft processes images, crops, QR codes, conversions, and text utilities entirely inside your browser's local sandbox memory using the HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript. Your private files never leave your computer unless you explicitly choose to save a project to your authenticated cloud account.

Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No, guest users can access all free standard tools without creating an account or logging in. Creating a free account allows you to save designs, store invoices, bookmark favorites, and access your cloud workspace across devices.

How does the UPI QR Generator work?

The UPI QR generator formats standard NPCI-compliant payment URI links (upi://pay?pa=...) into high-contrast QR codes. When scanned with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, it launches payment in the user's banking app. PixelCraft does not process or verify bank settlements.

What keyboard shortcuts are supported?

Press Ctrl + K or Cmd + K anywhere on the site to trigger the universal command palette and search tools or pages. Press Esc to close any active modal dialog. Press ? to view full shortcut mappings.

What happens if my Pro subscription expires?

We never delete or lock you out of your existing data. If your subscription expires, your account simply reverts to the Free tier. You can still view, download, and export all your previously created projects.
